# BALLY — interview word picker

A review tool for the BALLY × Daal & Bass interview (shot 5 Aug, Doppler Studio).

**Open it:** https://frompaulxam-gif.github.io/bally-interview/

## What it does

Three answers cut together as a multicam preview — O1, O2 and Q6 — with the full transcript
underneath, word by word.

- **Click any word** to cut it. It dims and strikes through. Click again to bring it back.
- **Drag across a phrase** to cut a whole run at once.
- **Double-click a word** to jump the video to that moment.
- The word he is currently saying **highlights in yellow** as the video plays.
- **Confirm selection** gives you the kept script, plus every cut section with timecodes
  into the video.
- **Copy shareable link** puts your choices in the URL — send that link back and it opens
  with everything exactly as you left it. Work also saves in the browser automatically.

## Notes on the cut

Only the picture switches between the two cameras; the audio runs continuously from the
A-cam, which is the only camera that had a working mic.

Timings come from speech recognition aligned onto the corrected transcript, so the wording
shown is the corrected version (the Punjabi terms are ones no recogniser gets right).
